The 4-Part Prompt Engineering Recipe

How to Give AI Clear Instructions

When humans talk to humans, we rely on tone of voice, body language, and shared common sense. When humans talk to AI, we must provide all 4 building blocks to get the exact output we want.

1

ROLE (Persona)

Who should the AI act as?

"Act as an expert math tutor for a 5th grader..."

Gives the AI the right mindset, tone, and knowledge level.

2

CONTEXT (Background)

What is the situation?

"...I am preparing for a science test about gravity on Mars..."

Helps AI understand why you are asking and what background applies.

3

TASK (Action)

What exact work should it do?

"...Explain how jump height changes on Mars compared to Earth..."

Uses strong action verbs: "List", "Summarize", "Compare", "Design".

4

CONSTRAINTS (Rules)

What are the limits or format?

"...In 3 bullet points, using simple words and 1 fun analogy."

Prevents overly long, off-topic, or confusing responses.

Classroom Exercise: Fix the Bad Prompt!

❌ Bad Vague Prompt

"Write a poem about space."

Result: Generates a generic, random rhyming poem that might be too long, babyish, or overly complex.

✅ Mastered Prompt (Role + Context + Task + Constraints)

"Act as a science communicator. Write a humorous 4-line poem for 10-year-olds explaining why astronauts float in microgravity. Do not use complex jargon."

Result: Perfectly targeted, highly engaging, exact length required!

Natural Intelligence (NI) Power

Why Natural Intelligence (NI) is Far More Important Than AI

AI has no mind, no feelings, and zero common sense. It is just code running fast calculation algorithms. Natural Intelligence (NI)—the human brain—is what gives AI purpose, rules, and direction.

1. NI Has Common Sense; AI Only Follows Patterns

Context & Judgment

When you say "make me a sandwich", your NI knows you mean food. AI takes the string literally and might answer "You are now a sandwich!" AI lacks human real-world understanding.

2. NI Verifies Truth; AI Can Hallucinate

Critical Thinking & Fact-Checking

AI makes up convincing-sounding fake facts (hallucinations). Only human Natural Intelligence can double-check outputs, evaluate evidence, and spot errors.

3. NI Is the Driver; AI Is Just the Engine

Vision & Purpose

A sports car engine is fast, but without a human driver holding the steering wheel, it goes nowhere or crashes. You provide the goals, questions, and creativity.

4. NI Possesses Empathy, Ethics & Care

Morals & Human Connection

AI doesn't care about fairness, kindness, or human feelings. Human NI ensures that technology is used responsibly and ethically to help people.
